# Pain Point Miner Context

Pain Point Miner analyzes Reddit discussions to discover SaaS opportunities, extracting validated user friction and computing commercial viability scores.

## Language

### Scoring & Metrics

**Pain Intensity**:
A calibrated 1–10 score reflecting the severity, bottleneck impact, and real-world friction of an extracted pain point.
_Avoid_: Rating, severity index, complaint level

**Monetization Score**:
A 1–10 score measuring commercial context, SaaS willingness-to-pay signals, or explicit budget mentions.
_Avoid_: Budget score, revenue rating, money score

**Urgency**:
A 1–10 score measuring how actively and immediately users are seeking a solution or relief.
_Avoid_: Priority, timeliness, speed score

**Market Maturity**:
A 1–10 score assessing existing competitive density and incumbent presence in the problem space.
_Avoid_: Saturation level, competitor score

**Validation Score**:
A normalized 0–100 signal derived from engagement metrics (upvotes, comment volume, thread mentions).
_Avoid_: Popularity score, viral score

**Opportunity Score**:
A composite 0–100 metric aggregating weighted pain dimensions, sentiment multipliers, feedback adjustments, and community validation.
_Avoid_: Report score, aggregate rating, rank
